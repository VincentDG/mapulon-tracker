Mapulon

Website for OBE Mapulon's tournaments

The Mapulon website stores information for OBE Mapulon's TFT tournaments. Oblation eSports is the premiere esports organization of the University of the Philippines Diliman, hosting seasonal competitive leagues.

About OBE Mapulon

Oblation eSports is the official eSports organization of UP - Diliman, establishing a legacy of collegiate excellence.

Mapulon, is the God of Seasons in Philippine mythology, overseeing growth, agriculture, and change.

Together, in the spirit of adaptation, OBE Mapulon hosts seasonal Teamfight Tactics leagues for players to compete, test their strategies, and climb the ranks.
